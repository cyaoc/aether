CELLS (sheet 17, the treasure chest game, fifth sheet): 1) idle: standing, both hands clasped in front of her skirt, a shy small smile at the viewer (already drawn in the canvas). 2) reaching after her treasure chest as it is carried off up into the sky to the viewer's RIGHT, too high to catch: standing on the tips of her toes on the ground line (her toes on that line, both heels lifted), her body turned three-quarter toward the viewer's RIGHT and leaning up toward the upper right; her LEFT arm (the one on the viewer's right) stretched out straight toward the upper right, its open hand with spread fingers reaching for something far away that is NOT drawn, the hand at about the height of the top of her head and well inside the cell; her RIGHT hand (on the viewer's left) a small fist held at her chest; her head tilted back looking up toward the upper right, eyes wide open and round looking up that way, eyebrows raised and drawn together in alarm, mouth wide open as if shouting "wait!", big tears welling at the rims of both eyes but not yet falling; her long hair swinging a little toward the left behind her. Her arms are as short as in cell 1 (a chibi's short arms); exactly two arms and two hands. 3) to 6): empty: leave these four cells perfectly flat green and draw nothing in them.
