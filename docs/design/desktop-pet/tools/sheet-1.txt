CELLS (sheet 1, everyday): 1) idle: standing, both hands clasped in front of her skirt, a shy small smile at the viewer. 2) wave: one small hand raised only beside her cheek in a timid little wave, shoulders slightly hunched, a shy smile. 3) bow: a polite bow, her upper body bent forward, hands together in front, eyes closed, a small smile. 4) cheer: both arms raised high and open, eyes shut in happy arcs, a big open smile, up on tiptoe. 5) surprised: both hands pressed to her cheeks, eyes wide and round, a small open mouth, leaning back a little. 6) dozing: sitting on the ground hugging her knees, head drooping forward, eyes closed, sleepy.
