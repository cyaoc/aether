CELLS (sheet 18, PK cheer sign): she is shy, so she holds up a sign instead of shouting. THE SIGN, the same sign in every cell from 2 to 6: a home-made landscape cardboard sign she cut out herself, cream-white with a soft pink marker border, slightly wobbly hand-cut edges and rounded corners; its face is completely BLANK: no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no drawing on it, a clean empty area for words to be added later. It is BIG for her and EXACTLY the same size and shape in every cell: about 255 px wide and 155 px tall at this canvas's scale (cell 1's girl is 360 px tall from the top of her hair to her shoe soles), so a little wider than her hair at its widest and a little shorter than her head; it faces the viewer flat and square, never turned, bent, shrunk or seen from the side. Exactly two arms and two hands, as short as in cell 1 (a chibi's short arms). The whole sign and the whole girl stay inside the cell. HELD UP OVER HER HEAD (cells 3 to 6): both arms raised up and a little outward exactly as high as in a cheer, her hands at the height of the top of her head and never higher; her two small hands are UNDER the sign's bottom edge, about as far apart as her head is wide, holding it up, so the sign rests flat above the top of her head with its bottom edge a little above her hair and not touching it, and it reaches out past her hands on both sides; her face fully visible under it. 1) idle: standing, both hands clasped in front of her skirt, a shy small smile at the viewer (already drawn in the canvas). 2) hiding behind the sign: standing, the big sign held upright in front of her, covering her from the nose down to her thighs; she holds it from behind with only the fingertips of both hands curled over its top edge, near the middle, about as far apart as her face is wide; only her eyes, eyebrows, bangs, the top of her head and her fingertips show above the sign's top edge, and her lower legs and shoes below it; eyes looking at the viewer shyly, a deep blush visible just above the sign. 3) sign up, earnest: held up over her head as described above, standing straight on the ground line; a red blush on both cheeks, eyes looking straight at the viewer, an earnest small smile with closed lips, eyebrows a little raised, shy but brave. 4) sign up, cheering: held up over her head as described above, standing on the ground line; mouth wide open in a happy cheer, eyes sparkling, cheeks blushing. 5) sign up, urging: held up over her head as described above, up on the tips of her toes (toes on the ground line, both heels lifted), body leaning a little forward toward the viewer; a WORRIED, URGENT face, never angry or fierce: her eyebrows lifted at their inner ends near the nose (worried brows sloping down toward the outer ends), eyes wide and round, mouth open in a small round "o" as if calling out "hurry!", cheeks blushing. 6) jumping with the sign up: the same hold and face as cell 4, but jumping in the air: both shoes off the ground, their soles about a hand's height above the ground line, knees bent with the lower legs kicked back a little, her skirt and hair lifted by the jump; her head and body the same size as in cell 4.
