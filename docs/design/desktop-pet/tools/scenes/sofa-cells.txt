SCENES (five sofas, five drawings of her, all LYING ON HER BACK along the sofa's seat: her head on the cream pillow at the RIGHT armrest (the viewer's right), propped up a little so her face is turned three-quarters toward the viewer and both her eyes show, her body along the seat and her legs toward the left armrest, her whole body on the seat, nothing hanging down to the ground). TOP MIDDLE, playing: holding the phone sideways (landscape) in both hands above her chest in front of her face, its pink back toward the viewer, thumbs on its screen, the knee nearer the viewer bent up with the other leg crossed over it and that foot in the air, eyes wide and focused on the screen, a determined little smile. TOP RIGHT, won: the hand nearer the viewer pumping a fist up in the air, the other hand holding the phone up, both legs kicking up in the air with joy, eyes closed in happy arcs, a big open laughing mouth. BOTTOM LEFT, lost: still holding the phone sideways above her chest in both hands, frozen in shock, eyes turned into blank round white dots with no pupils, her mouth a small open line, both legs lying flat. BOTTOM MIDDLE, the phone fell on her face: the phone lying flat ON her face, covering her nose and eyes, its pink back up; both her hands jerked up open beside her head in surprise, both legs jerked up stiffly. BOTTOM RIGHT, ouch: the hand nearer the viewer covering her red nose, eyes squeezed shut with tears at their corners, a wobbly pouting mouth, the phone lying on her tummy under her other hand, both legs lying flat.
