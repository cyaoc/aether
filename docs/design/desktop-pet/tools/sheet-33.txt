CELLS (sheet 33, the Miao gu-keeper cosplay). THE POT: the small round silver pot of the design sheet, with its domed lid and knob. 1) idle, EVERYDAY outfit: standing, both hands clasped in front of her skirt, a shy small smile at the viewer (already drawn in the canvas). 2) ready, COSPLAY outfit: standing, the lidded pot held in her LEFT hand (the viewer's right) at her side, her RIGHT hand relaxed, a sweet smile. 3) pose, COSPLAY outfit: standing, her head tilted to one side so the silver tassels of her crown swing, her RIGHT hand raised beside her cheek with thumb and middle finger touching in an elegant orchid-finger gesture, the pot in her LEFT hand at her hip, a mysterious little smile with one eye winking. 4) holding the pot, COSPLAY outfit: standing, the lidded pot in front of her chest in BOTH hands, her LEFT hand under it, her RIGHT hand on its lid, leaning forward a little with a sly smile. 5) lid off, COSPLAY outfit: standing, the pot in her LEFT hand held in front of her chest with its open mouth up, her RIGHT hand holding the lid up beside her head (no higher than her forehead), looking at the pot with an excited open smile. 6) finger up, COSPLAY outfit: standing, her RIGHT index finger raised beside her face at eye height, gazing at its tip with a soft smile (draw NO butterfly: it is added later), the lidded pot back in her LEFT hand at her hip.
