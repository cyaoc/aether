CELLS (sheet 20, the 扭蛋 daily, second sheet). The capsule is ONE round capsule-toy ball about the size of her fist, its top half clear pale pink and its bottom half white, a thin seam between the halves. 1) idle: standing, both hands clasped in front of her skirt, a shy small smile at the viewer (already drawn in the canvas). 2) kneeling and wailing: sitting on her heels on the ground with her knees together, leaning a little forward, both fists rubbing her eyes, mouth wide open in a loud wail, big streams of tears running down both cheeks; her hands are empty. 3) wiping her tears: standing, wiping one eye with the back of her right hand (on the viewer's left), her left hand in a loose fist at her side, teary eyes, a wobbly brave little smile, sniffling. 4) a bright idea, pointing home: standing, her body turned slightly toward the viewer's LEFT, her right arm (on the viewer's left) stretched out pointing to the viewer's LEFT at shoulder height with her index finger, her left hand in a small fist in front of her chest, eyes wide and shining, eyebrows raised, an excited open smile. 5) rubbing her hands, waiting: standing, both hands together in front of her chest rubbing her palms, looking toward the viewer's LEFT with eager sparkling eyes, a gleeful smile. 6) looking at the capsule: standing, holding the capsule up in front of her face with both hands, looking at it with sparkling eyes and a delighted small open smile.
