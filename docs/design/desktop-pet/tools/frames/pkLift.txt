Sitting on the chest laughing and pointing at her, it gets up to carry the chest off: it rises, takes its hook rope in both fists and pulls it tight, the rope hanging straight down to the chest below it. Frame 2: its pointing arm coming down, still grinning, its body lifting and leaning forward, both stubby arms reaching down in front of it to gather the rope.
