Frame 2: leaning a little toward the viewer, the ski tips turning slightly toward the viewer, the scarf flapping up. Frame 3: centred again like frame 1, the scarf flapping down. Frame 4: leaning a little away from the viewer, the ski tips turning slightly away. Her boots never leave the skis.
