In the cycling outfit, after crashing she sits up beside the fallen bicycle and puts a plaster on her own bare scraped RIGHT knee, which is the knee on the viewer's LEFT, the knee that hit the ground. The bicycle lies on its side to her right exactly where it fell in frame 1, and nobody touches it: it is still lying there in frame 4. Frame 2: pushing herself up from the ground, dizzy, one hand going to that knee. Frame 3: sitting up with her knees up, looking at the bare scraped knee and peeling a small plaster. The plaster only ever goes on that bare knee, never on her arms, elbows, clothes or shoes.
