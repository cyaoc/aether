Up on her toes reaching after her treasure chest as it flies off to the upper right, she cannot catch it: her knees give way and she drops to kneel, bursting into tears. Frame 2: already clearly lower than frame 1: her heels down flat on the ground and her knees bent deeply in a half squat, her body sinking; her stretched LEFT arm (the one on the viewer's right) sinking from the upper right to point out to the right at shoulder height, still open-handed; her RIGHT fist (on the viewer's left) at her chest; her eyes still open but crumpling, the first tears falling. Frame 3: she has dropped all the way: her knees already on the ground and her bottom sitting back toward her heels, almost exactly the kneeling pose of frame 4, turned three-quarter to the right; her left arm reaching out to the right at shoulder height with the hand open, her right fist at her chest; her eyes now squeezed shut in two curved lines with big tears streaming down, as in frame 4, mouth opening wide to wail. Exactly two hands in every frame.
