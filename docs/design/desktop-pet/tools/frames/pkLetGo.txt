Flying along holding its hook rope with a heavy chest hanging from it, something gives it a terrible fright: it lets go of the rope and jumps up. Frame 2: its body jerking up and tensing, eyes flying wide open, its stubby arms coming apart and opening as the rope slips out of them (the rope is not drawn). Frame 3: jumping up and stretching taller, both empty arms going up toward its cheeks, mouth opening into a small round O with its fang showing, the loot sack swinging up behind it.
