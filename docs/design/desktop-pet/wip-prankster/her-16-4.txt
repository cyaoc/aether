SCENE (one chest, one drawing of her): BOTTOM LEFT, the open chest, pulling it by its handle after a lightning zap. A COMPACT pose: she squats very low and UPRIGHT, her back straight up and her head right above her heels, NOT leaning back: heels dug into the ground close to the chest, knees bent deep, both arms stretched forward and DOWN toward the lower right, BOTH fists closed tight around the gold ring handle, one fist beside the other, her fingers wrapped through the ring; the ring stays where the canvas has it, level, its right end still on the chest; her fists are low, lower than her knees, and they are the farthest thing to the right of her. Her long black hair stands out in a frizzy static puff all around her head, with a few small soot smudges on her cheeks, as in reference sheet A's bottom-middle cell, but the puff is SMALL and tight to her head: it sticks out no more than a hand's width beyond her head on any side. Eyes wide, round and dazed, mouth a small wobbly line. From the leftmost tip of her hair to her fists she is no wider than one and a half times the standing girl, hair included.
