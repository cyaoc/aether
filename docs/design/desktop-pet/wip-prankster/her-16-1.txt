SCENES (three chests, three drawings of her): TOP RIGHT, the closed chest, lunging to grab its handle: three-quarter view turned to the viewer's RIGHT, she has dived forward and down at the chest, body bent low, one foot stepping forward on the ground and the other behind, both arms stretched down and forward, and BOTH hands have just closed on the gold ring handle on the chest's left side, low, at the height of her shins; eyes fierce and determined, mouth open in a shout. BOTTOM LEFT, an open chest, pulling it by its handle with all her might: three-quarter view, squatting very low, her whole body leaning far back toward the viewer's LEFT, heels dug into the ground, knees bent deep, both arms stretched straight out and DOWN toward the lower right, BOTH fists closed tight around the gold ring handle, one fist beside the other, her fingers wrapped through the ring; the ring stays where the canvas has it, level, its right end still on the chest; her fists are low, lower than her knees, right in front of her shins, and they are the farthest thing to the right of her; eyes squeezed shut, teeth clenched with effort. BOTTOM RIGHT, the other open chest, the same pull after a lightning zap: exactly the pose of the bottom-left drawing, both fists closed tight around that chest's gold ring handle in the same way, but her long black hair stands out in one big frizzy static puff all around her head, with a few small soot smudges on her cheeks, exactly as in reference sheet A's bottom-middle cell; eyes wide, round and dazed, mouth a small wobbly line.
