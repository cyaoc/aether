CELLS (sheet 13, the treasure chest game): 1) idle: standing, both hands clasped in front of her skirt, a shy small smile at the viewer (already drawn in the canvas). 2) bonked on the head: something has just dropped on her head: standing with her knees slightly bent and her shoulders hunched up, both hands pressed flat on the top of her head, eyes squeezed shut tight, mouth a small wavy "ouch" line, one tiny tear at the corner of one eye. 3) flattened: lying sprawled flat on her stomach along the ground line like a starfish, both arms and both legs spread out, her face turned toward the viewer with dizzy spiral eyes and a wobbly mouth. 4) tug of war: three-quarter view, pulling a rope with all her might: her whole body leaning far back toward the viewer's LEFT, heels dug in on the ground line, knees bent, both arms stretched out toward the viewer's RIGHT with both hands gripping one thick tan rope, the rope running straight on from her hands to the viewer's right for about the length of her forearm and ending in the air well inside the cell; eyes squeezed shut, teeth clenched with effort. 5) tug of war after a lightning zap: exactly the pose and the rope of cell 4, but her long black hair stands out in one big frizzy static puff all around her head, a few small soot smudges on her cheeks, eyes wide, round and dazed, mouth a small wobbly line; she still grips the rope with both hands. 6) dragged along the ground: lying on her stomach stretched out along the ground line, her head toward the viewer's RIGHT and her feet toward the viewer's left, both arms stretched forward with both hands gripping the same thick tan rope, which runs on to the viewer's right for about the length of her forearm and ends in the air well inside the cell; her face lifted toward the viewer, cheeks puffed, eyes teary but stubborn.
