Her long black hair stands out in a frizzy static puff all around her head, with a few small soot smudges on her cheeks, as in reference sheet A's bottom-middle cell, but the puff is SMALL and tight to her head: it sticks out no more than a hand's width beyond her head on any side. Eyes wide, round and dazed, mouth a small wobbly line. ALL of her, the whole puff included, is inside the canvas with a clear green margin on the left: nothing of her touches or is cut by the canvas's left edge, and she is NOT drawn smaller to fit: she is exactly the standing girl's scale.
